# Audiences by ecological level (v0.1)

_Generated by `scripts/build.py` from [`audiences.csv`](audiences.csv). Draft; nothing here has been reviewed by a person yet._

Levels: McLeroy, Bibeau, Steckler & Glanz (1988), as presented in NCI *Theory at a Glance* (2005), Table 1. Populations map to MeSH (NLM); roles to LRMI and schema.org. Full citations: [REFERENCES.md](../REFERENCES.md).

Each audience is placed at the level where it **acts**: a parent acts on a child's sleep through the relationship (level 2), a principal through the school's rules (level 3). **Population** entries (life stages and situations) describe who a resource is *about*; combine one with a role, e.g. *parents and caregivers* + *teens*.

62 concepts. Facets: role = a person acting in a role; organization = a kind of organization; group = a collective; population = who it is about.
