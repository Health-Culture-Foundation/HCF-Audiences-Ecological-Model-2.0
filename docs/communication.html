Communication direction

Who is talking to whom. This is not a level of influence: pair it with an audience role, which says which professional and so which level.

SleepLiteracy's Pro2Pro tag means professional to professional, a very large category. It is split here by what the professional does with the resource.

DirectionMeansSleepLiteracy tag
Professional to public
public-facing, for the public, for patients
Made by professionals or organizations for people to use about their own or their family's health.
e.g. A CDC page on how much sleep teens need.
Most health content. Pair with level-1 and level-2 audiences.
Professional to professional
pro2pro, for professionals, for my work, B2B
Made for people to use in their work: to practise, teach, run a program or pass information on. A very large category; the narrower concepts say what the professional does with it.
The audience roles (clinicians, health communicators, school and campus leaders...) say which professional and therefore which level. Kalman, 22 Oct: a lot of communications fall into this category.
audience/pro2pro (exact)
↳ Guidance for practice
guidelines, clinical recommendations, standards, position statements
Tells professionals what to do in their own practice.
e.g. ACSM guidelines for prescribing exercise; AASM sleep duration recommendations.
SleepLiteracy example: ACSM 'Guidelines for prescribing exercise in medicine'.
↳ Materials to pass on
handouts, toolkits, editable slides, campaign kits, patient fact sheets, pro-to-pro-to-public
Made for professionals to hand on, present or adapt for the people they serve. Two audiences: the professional who uses it and the public who ends up reading it.
e.g. World Sleep Day toolkit with editable slides; patient fact sheets for clinicians to give out.
Tag both directions' audiences, e.g. clinicians + the person themselves. MeSH Patient Education Handout is a publication type. SleepLiteracy examples: World Sleep Day resources; Sleep Health Foundation fact sheets.
↳ Training and continuing education
CME, CPD, courses, continuing education
Teaches professionals new skills or knowledge, often for credit.
e.g. NIOSH's online shift-work and sleep training for nurses.
↳ Research and evidence
studies, reviews, data, reports
Reports what was studied and found, mainly for other researchers and evidence users.
e.g. A systematic review of school start times and teen sleep.
Most journal articles. The sleep-research note shows these tag populations, not actors.
↳ Running programs and changing systems
implementation guides, program manuals, policy templates, how-to for organizations
Helps professionals set up a program or change how their organization works.
e.g. A guide for campuses on moving early exams.
Pairs with level-3 and level-4 audiences.
Professional to decision-maker
policy briefs, testimony, advocacy materials
Made to inform or persuade people who decide policy.
e.g. A brief for state legislators on school start times.
Pairs with level-5 audiences and the proposed advocacy-phase facet (research/advocacy-frameworks).
Peer to peer
lived experience, community voices, support groups
Made by members of the public for each other, from lived experience.
e.g. A narcolepsy support community's tips for college.
Public to professional
patient voice, community input, lived-experience input
People and communities telling professionals and decision-makers what they need.
e.g. A survey of shift workers' sleep needs shared with employers.
Rarely tagged anywhere; useful for participatory and policy work.
